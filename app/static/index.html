help_outline Quick Start Guide

1. Set up your spin system
Define the electron spin S, g-tensor, and any nuclei with their hyperfine couplings.
2. Choose a simulator
Use garlic() for liquid/solution EPR (fast tumbling, S=1/2).
Use pepper() for solid-state/powder EPR (any S).
3. Set experiment parameters
Enter the microwave frequency and magnetic field range matching your spectrometer.
4. Simulate!
Press the Simulate button. The spectrum and energy level diagram update automatically.
